\section{Ion-train Raman System}
In this section, we will discuss an ion-train system with transverse Raman beams. Ion trapped in a linear Paul trap. Three transverse Laser beams are applied to the ion that willing to drive the Raman transition. Red and blue sideband transitions are driven by the Raman beams. Multi-motional modes are considered simutaneously. The cutoff of motional modes is set to be $8$, which gives an error of $10^{-5}$ in the simulation. Since classical computer can not handle the large Hilbert space, the limit of the number of mode could be considered simutaneously is $\approx 9$(equivalent to $29$ qubits). For ion train longer than $9$ ions, we only consider nearest $9$ modes.
